About me

I am a mathematics researcher, mainly interested in the interplay between number theory and additive combinatorics, with occasional forays into discrete geometry. My work combines analytic and algebraic techniques to tackle problems of structure and counting in finite and infinite sets.

Before my current position, I completed my PhD at the University of Example (2020-2024) under the supervision of Dr. First Last, and held a postdoctoral position at the Example Institute. More details are available in my CV and in my list of publications.

Research areas

Analytic number theory

Distribution of prime numbers, L-functions and applications of sieve methods.

Additive combinatorics

Structure of sumsets, Freiman-type theorems and arithmetic progressions in dense sets.

Discrete geometry

Incidence problems and extremal configurations in finite spaces.
